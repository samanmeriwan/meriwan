# Month-End Checklist - Meriwan Trading FZCO

Owner: Accountant. Sign-off: Finance Manager. Target: done by the 5th working day.

## 1. Sales and customers
- [ ] All deliveries of the month validated (Inventory > Deliveries, filter "Ready" / "Waiting" = none left for the month)
- [ ] Every delivered sales order invoiced (Sales > To Invoice > Orders to Invoice)
- [ ] Customer payments registered and matched (Accounting > Bank > reconcile)
- [ ] Customer advances (214000) reviewed: each advance linked to an open order or applied to its invoice
- [ ] Aged Receivable reviewed; follow-ups sent for overdue customers
- [ ] Credit notes for returns posted with a reason

## 2. Purchases and vendors
- [ ] All receipts of the month validated
- [ ] Vendor bills entered for every receipt (Purchase > To Bill)
- [ ] Freight, customs and clearing bills entered and **landed costs validated** for the related receipts
- [ ] Landed Cost Clearing (213000) balance explained (should be zero or only open shipments)
- [ ] Vendor advances reviewed against open purchase orders
- [ ] Aged Payable reviewed; payments scheduled

## 3. Bank and cash
- [ ] Bank statements imported for BNK1 (AED) and BNK2 (USD) up to the last day
- [ ] All bank lines reconciled; no open items in "Outstanding receipts / payments"
- [ ] Cash (CSH1) and Petty Cash (PCSH) counted and matched to Odoo
- [ ] USD and other currency rates up to date (Accounting > Configuration > Currencies)

## 4. Inventory
- [ ] Stock count of fast movers (or full count at year end); adjustments applied with a reason
- [ ] Stock valuation report = Inventory account (110100) on the balance sheet
- [ ] Quality Hold and Damaged locations reviewed; write-offs approved
- [ ] Expired reservations on Order Forms released or extended

## 5. Accounting close
- [ ] Accruals and prepayments posted (rent, salaries, utilities)
- [ ] Fixed asset additions recorded
- [ ] Trial balance reviewed; suspense / clearing accounts at zero
- [ ] Profit and Loss and Balance Sheet reviewed by the Finance Manager
- [ ] **Lock date set** (Accounting > Configuration > Settings > Lock Dates) to the last day of the month

## 6. Reports to management
- [ ] Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet, Aged Receivable / Payable
- [ ] Sales by customer / product, margin analysis (Order Form > Margin Analysis)
- [ ] Stock value by category, slow-moving items
